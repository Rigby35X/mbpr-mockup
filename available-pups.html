Meet “The 17!”

Aust. Cattle Dog / Mix · Born May 5, 2026 · Medium at grown, 25–50 lbs

6 of 17 pups still looking

Their mama slipped out of her yard and came back with quite the surprise — a litter of 7 girls and 10 boys. Their family turned to the County for help, and after Mama was spayed, the County asked Mission Bay Puppy Rescue to find loving homes for all 17 of her babies. These playful, curious pups are growing more confident every day. Seventeen little lives, seventeen fresh starts, seventeen forever families waiting to be found.
