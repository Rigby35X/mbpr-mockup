How It All Started

It Began With 10 Puppies On Our Front Lawn

30 years ago, while searching for a rescue dog of our own, we were unexpectedly asked to foster. Our journey began with a family of 10 puppies — and while they played on our front lawn, passersby couldn’t resist their charm.

After years of working with rescue organizations and placing hundreds of puppies, we made it official. In December 2022, Mission Bay Puppy Rescue became a 501(c)(3) nonprofit.
